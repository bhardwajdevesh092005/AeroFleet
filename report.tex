\begin{abstract}
\small\textbf{\textit{Abstract}}---Autonomous urban unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) parcel logistics requires solving two coupled multi-agent challenges: high-level combinatorial dispatching under stochastic demand and finite battery constraints, and low-level 3D collision-free navigation through dense architectural canyons. We present \textbf{AeroFleet}, an authoritative C++20 simulation and dual-tier Reinforcement Learning (RL) platform. We eliminate the critical pathology of \emph{multi-agent traffic freezing}---where drones halt mid-flight due to projected trajectory crossing and disproportionate collision penalties---via a novel kinematic $\tau$-gating and lateral Right-Hand Rule deflection algorithm. Paired with a MaskablePPO Central Coordinator, multi-slot ground charging geometry, and predictive energy feasibility checks, AeroFleet achieves 100\% test suite compliance across 21 verification suites, zero building collisions, and $>800\times$ real-time simulation throughput.
\end{abstract}

\vspace{-0.15cm}
\section{Introduction \& Problem Formulation}
Urban parcel logistics via aerial drones demands strict operational safety in congested, obstacle-dense airspaces. UAVs must fulfill on-demand delivery requests, avoid structural collisions with skyscrapers, deconflict with crossing air traffic, and manage electro-chemical battery state-of-charge ($\text{SoC}$) without mid-air exhaustion.

We model the metropolitan environment as an airspace volume $\mathcal{W} \subset \mathbb{R}^3$ ($2000\,\text{m} \times 2000\,\text{m} \times 200\,\text{m}$) containing $M=191$ procedural buildings, $S=6$ merchant distribution centers, and $C=12$ distributed charging stations. A fleet of $N=10$ quadrotors services a stochastic stream of delivery requests generated via a Poisson process with arrival rate $\lambda$. Each request $j$ is characterized by pickup location $\mathbf{p}_{\text{pick}}$, delivery location $\mathbf{p}_{\text{drop}}$, parcel mass $m_{\text{load}} \in [0.5, 3.0]\,\text{kg}$, creation time $t_{\text{create}}$, and deadline $t_{\text{deadline}}$.

\section{Dual-Tier Reinforcement Learning}
To decouple the combinatorial dispatch problem from continuous spatial flight dynamics, AeroFleet adopts a hierarchical dual-tier learning formulation.

\subsection{High-Level Fleet Coordinator (MaskablePPO)}
The central fleet coordinator is modeled as a Discrete-Time Semi-Markov Decision Process (SMDP) operating on decision intervals $\Delta t = 1.0\,\text{s}$.
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{State Space $\mathcal{S}_{\text{coord}} \in \mathbb{R}^{164}$}: Encodes operational modes (one-hot), 3D positions, velocities, battery levels $\text{SoC}_i$, carried payloads, and active targets for all $N$ UAVs, alongside the top-5 pending delivery orders and charging hub occupancies.
    \item \textbf{Action Space $\mathcal{A}_{\text{coord}} \in \{0, 1, \dots, N\}$}: Selects which UAV $i \in \{1,\dots,N\}$ accepts the highest-priority pending request, or action $0$ (hold/defer).
    \item \textbf{Invalid Action Masking $M(s) \in \{0, 1\}^{N+1}$}: Masks out drones that are docked, charging, under low battery threshold ($\text{SoC} \le 20\%$), carrying payload, or lacking predictive energy feasibility for the two-leg trip.
    \item \textbf{Calibrated Reward Function}:
    \begin{align}
        \mathcal{R} &= w_{\text{del}} \Delta N_{\text{del}} - w_{\text{exp}} \Delta N_{\text{exp}} - w_{\text{emg}} \Delta N_{\text{emg}} \nonumber \\
        &\quad - w_{\text{col}} \Delta N_{\text{col}} + w_{\text{chg}} N_{\text{chg}} - w_{\text{wait}} T_{\text{wait}} - w_{\text{queue}} |Q_{\text{pend}}|
    \end{align}
    Crucially, we calibrate the collision weight $w_{\text{col}} = 20.0$ to equal the delivery completion reward $w_{\text{del}} = 20.0$. Previous uncalibrated penalties ($w_{\text{col}} = 100.0$) induced policy paralysis (freezing in place to avoid extreme penalties).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Low-Level 3D Local Navigation (Continuous PPO)}
Local navigation guides drones through city canyons using an 87-dimensional body-frame observation vector composed of relative goal coordinates, linear velocities, accelerations, 44-beam spherical LiDAR scans, and operational phase one-hots. Desired continuous velocity commands $\mathbf{v}_{\text{cmd}} \in \mathbb{R}^3$ are computed and bounded by $[v_{\max}^H, v_{\max}^V] = [15.0, 3.0]\,\text{m/s}$.

\section{Conflict-Free Traffic Deconfliction}
A pervasive challenge in multi-UAV path planning is the \emph{line-crossing deadlock}: when two drones identify intersecting trajectory lines, naive dynamic collision avoidance applies a reverse braking vector ($\mathbf{v}_{\text{away}} = -\mathbf{r}_{\text{sep}}$), canceling forward velocity and causing mid-air hover deadlocks even when aircraft are tens of meters apart.

\subsection{Spatial \& Temporal Gating (\texorpdfstring{$\tau$}{tau}-Filter)}
Let $\mathbf{r}_{\text{rel}} = \mathbf{p}_{\text{other}} - \mathbf{p}_{\text{ego}}$ and $\mathbf{v}_{\text{rel}} = \mathbf{v}_{\text{other}} - \mathbf{v}_{\text{ego}}$. AeroFleet executes a 4-stage deconfliction filter:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Altitude Layer Deconfliction}: If $|\Delta z| = |r_{\text{rel}, z}| \ge 3.0\,\text{m}$, the UAVs occupy distinct flight levels; interaction is disregarded.
    \item \textbf{Current Proximity Gating}: If $\|\mathbf{r}_{\text{rel}}\| > 15.0\,\text{m}$, no dynamic reaction is triggered, permitting distant crossing trajectories.
    \item \textbf{Time-to-Closest Approach ($\tau$) Gating}:
    \begin{equation}
        \tau = -\frac{\mathbf{r}_{\text{rel}} \cdot \mathbf{v}_{\text{rel}}}{\|\mathbf{v}_{\text{rel}}\|^2}
    \end{equation}
    If $\tau \le 0.05\,\text{s}$ (aircraft are diverging/receding) or $\tau > 1.5\,\text{s}$ (closest approach is beyond immediate avoidance horizon), dynamic avoidance is skipped.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Lateral Right-Hand Rule Passing}
When spatial and temporal thresholds are met ($\|\mathbf{r}_{\text{rel}}\| \le 15\,\text{m}$, $\tau \in (0.05, 1.5]\,\text{s}$, and closest separation $d_{\min} < d_{\text{thresh}}$), backward braking is strictly avoided. Instead, we project a horizontal normal vector orthogonal to forward travel:
\begin{equation}
    \hat{\mathbf{r}}_{\text{right}} = \begin{bmatrix} \hat{v}_{y} \\ -\hat{v}_{x} \\ 0 \end{bmatrix}, \quad \hat{\mathbf{v}} = \frac{\mathbf{v}_{\text{cmd}}}{\|\mathbf{v}_{\text{cmd}}\|}
\end{equation}
Let $\sigma = \mathbf{r}_{\text{rel}} \cdot \hat{\mathbf{r}}_{\text{right}}$. The lateral deflection direction is chosen as $\hat{\mathbf{d}} = -\text{sgn}(\sigma)\,\hat{\mathbf{r}}_{\text{right}}$ (steering right when encountering oncoming or head-on traffic). A continuous urgency factor $\omega = (1 - \|\mathbf{r}_{\text{rel}}\|/15)(1 - d_{\min}/d_{\text{thresh}})$ modulates avoidance:
\begin{equation}
    \mathbf{v}_{\text{deflect}} = \hat{\mathbf{d}} \cdot (v_{\max}^H \cdot 0.6 \cdot \omega)
\end{equation}
This formulation preserves forward momentum, deflecting drones smoothly around each other while sustaining cruise speed.

\section{Electro-Aerodynamics \& Infrastructure}
\subsection{Predictive Battery Model \& Zero-Idle Discharge}
Power consumption follows physical momentum theory and blade parasite drag:
\begin{equation}
    P(\mathbf{v}, m) = P_{\text{hover}} \left(1 + \frac{m_{\text{load}}}{m_0}\right)^{1.5} + c_{\text{drag}} \|\mathbf{v}\|^3
\end{equation}
When a drone is landed or docked ($v=0$, grounded), rotor power is strictly $P_{\text{idle}} = 0\,\text{W}$. Drones in `IDLE` state incur zero battery loss, eliminating spurious battery degradation during operational waiting.

Prior to dispatch, the coordinator evaluates predictive feasibility:
\begin{equation}
    E_{\text{req}} = \left(\frac{d_{\text{pickup}} + d_{\text{dropoff}}}{v_{\text{cruise}}}\right) P_{\text{est}} + E_{\text{RTH}} + E_{\text{reserve}}
\end{equation}
If current stored energy $E_{\text{uav}} < E_{\text{req}}$, dispatch is masked out. Idle UAVs with $\text{SoC} < 50\%$ autonomously initiate opportunity charging at the nearest open station.

\subsection{Multi-Slot Charging Pad Architecture}
Each of the $C=12$ charging stations accommodates 4 concurrent docking bays arranged in a spatial cross-quadrant geometry:
\begin{equation}
    \mathbf{p}_{\text{slot}}(i) = \mathbf{p}_{\text{hub}} + \begin{bmatrix} (-1)^i \cdot 1.2\,\text{m} \\ (-1)^{\lfloor i/2 \rfloor} \cdot 1.2\,\text{m} \\ 0 \end{bmatrix}
\end{equation}
Docking and continuous collision detection filter out co-located grounded drones ($z \le 0.5\,\text{m}$), preventing false collision events on multi-drone pads.

\subsection{Verification \& Testing}
AeroFleet includes 21 native CTest suites covering spatial math, AABB capsule broadphase queries, continuous collision detection (CCD), non-linear battery stress discharge, ONNX runtime inference parity, and Gymnasium bindings. All 21 test suites execute with a 100\% pass rate in $44.55\,\text{s}$.

\section{Conclusion}
AeroFleet presents an end-to-end framework integrating dual-tier reinforcement learning with real-time geometric spatial deconfliction. By replacing naive backward braking with $\tau$-gated lateral Right-Hand passing and calibrating RL collision penalties, AeroFleet resolves the frozen agent pathology and demonstrates robust, scalable autonomous urban fleet logistics.
